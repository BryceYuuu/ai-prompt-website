## September 26 catalog expansion

The six added image cases (21, 35, 60, 64, 86, 98) retain their original image bytes and per-case attribution files. Their full upstream `case.yml` snapshots are stored beside those files. The 12 added Fabric tasks include chapter extraction, structured outlining, pull-request descriptions, Terraform impact review, hypothesis testing, cross-source patterns, concept maps, answer diagnosis, effort estimates, presentation outlines, goal review and instructional checklists. Adaptations focus on concrete inputs, checkable outputs and explicit unknowns; no card is represented as individually model-tested. Repository star counts are project-level observations on September 26, 2026, not popularity scores for individual templates. Public API evidence is committed in `tools/curate/sources/*-repository-2026-09-26.json`.
